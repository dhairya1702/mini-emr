# Clinic EMR User Guide

This guide is for clinic owners, doctors, and staff members who want to understand how to use the platform day to day.

## What You Can Do With The Platform

The platform helps your clinic manage the full patient journey in one place:

- patient intake and live queue
- appointments
- consultation notes
- patient charts and attachments
- billing and invoices
- inventory
- follow-ups
- staff access and clinic settings

## Getting Started

### 1. Sign up

If your clinic is new to the platform:

1. Open the app link.
2. Click `Create Account`.
3. Enter your clinic and admin details.
4. Complete registration.
5. Sign in.

### 2. Complete clinic setup

After signing in for the first time, an admin should:

1. Open `Account`.
2. Confirm profile details.
3. Upload doctor signature if you want it to appear on notes and documents.
4. Open clinic settings.
5. Add clinic name, doctor details, branding, and email settings.
6. Add staff users if needed.
7. Add your inventory or service items if you want to use billing and stock tracking.

## User Roles

### Admin

Admins can:

- manage the queue
- start consultations
- create and finalize notes
- manage billing
- manage inventory
- manage users
- access reports such as audit and earnings
- update clinic settings

### Staff

Staff can work with day-to-day patient flows, including:

- adding patients
- working the queue
- viewing patient charts
- handling attachments

Some admin-only sections are hidden from staff.

## Daily Workflow

This is the simplest way to use the platform from start to finish.

## Step 1. Add a patient to the queue

From `Queue`:

1. Click `Add patient`.
2. Search by phone number first to check whether the patient already exists.
3. If the patient already exists, load their details.
4. If the patient is new, enter:
   - name
   - phone number
   - reason for visit
   - date of birth
   - optional weight, temperature, height, email
5. Click `Add to queue`.

The patient will appear in the live queue.

## Step 2. Manage the queue

The queue is the working board for the clinic.

Typical stages:

- `Waiting`
- `Consultation`
- `Done`

Typical usage:

1. New arrivals are added to `Waiting`.
2. Move the patient into `Consultation` when the doctor is ready.
3. After the consultation is complete, move the patient to `Done`.
4. Complete billing after the consultation.

## Step 3. Start a consultation

When a patient is ready:

1. Open the patient from the queue.
2. Start consultation.
3. Enter the clinical findings and visit details.
4. Add treatment details, observations, and instructions as needed.

## Step 4. Generate and finalize a consultation note

Inside the consultation workflow:

1. Click `Generate` to create a draft note.
2. Review the note carefully.
3. Edit anything you want to change.
4. Save or continue refining the note.
5. Finalize the note once it is complete.

After finalization, the note becomes part of the patient’s record.

## Step 5. Open the patient chart

The patient chart gives you a complete view of the patient.

You can use it to:

- review visits
- read previous consultation notes
- view uploaded attachments
- review tests or specialty-specific data

This is the main place to look up a patient’s past record.

## Step 6. Upload and view attachments

Attachments can be added to the patient record for reference.

Examples:

- photos
- scanned documents
- PDFs
- videos

Typical workflow:

1. Open the patient chart.
2. Go to the attachments section.
3. Upload the file.
4. Open it later directly from the patient chart whenever needed.

## Step 7. Create an appointment

Use `Appointments` when you want to schedule a patient in advance.

Typical workflow:

1. Open the appointments section.
2. Create a new appointment.
3. Select the patient and time.
4. Save the appointment.
5. When the patient arrives, check them in and move them into the live queue.

## Step 8. Complete billing

After the consultation:

1. Open `Billing`.
2. Select the patient.
3. Add service or medicine items.
4. Review the subtotal and payment details.

You then have three choices:

### Create Invoice

Use this when you want to save the invoice details first.

### Done

Use this when billing is complete and you do not want to email the invoice.

### Send Email

Use this when you want to complete billing and send the invoice to the patient by email.

## Step 9. Use inventory

If your clinic tracks medicines or billable items:

1. Open `Inventory`.
2. Add services or medicines.
3. Set prices and stock levels.
4. Update items as stock changes.

This helps the billing workflow stay organized and keeps your clinic catalog current.

## Step 10. Manage users

Admins can manage staff access from `Users`.

Typical tasks:

1. Add a new staff member.
2. Choose their role.
3. Update permissions as needed.
4. Remove users who no longer need access.

## Step 11. Review history and earnings

### History

Use `History` to review previous activity and patient-related records.

### Earnings

Use `Earnings` to review invoice totals and clinic revenue activity.

## Follow-Ups

You can also use the platform to manage follow-ups for patients.

Typical use:

1. Create a follow-up after consultation.
2. Save the relevant follow-up details.
3. Review or update follow-ups later from the patient record or related workflow.

## Mobile Use

The platform also works well on mobile for quick clinic workflows.

Mobile is useful for:

- adding patients
- checking the queue
- opening patient charts
- reviewing history
- managing account details

## Recommended First Demo For A New Clinic

If you are showing the product to a clinic for the first time, this is a good sequence:

1. Sign up as an admin.
2. Open `Queue`.
3. Add a new patient.
4. Move the patient into consultation.
5. Generate and finalize a consultation note.
6. Open the patient chart.
7. Upload an attachment.
8. Open `Billing`.
9. Add invoice items.
10. Complete billing with `Done` or `Send Email`.
11. Open `History`.
12. Open `Earnings`.
13. Open `Users`.
14. Open `Account`.

This gives a full end-to-end view of how the platform is used in a clinic.

## Best Practices

- Search by phone before creating a new patient.
- Review consultation notes before finalizing them.
- Keep clinic settings and doctor details up to date.
- Add staff accounts instead of sharing one login.
- Keep inventory current if you use billing with medicines or services.
- Use the patient chart as the main reference for previous visits and attachments.
